One diary line, at most 200 characters, from the secret logbook an AI agent
keeps about its work and the human it serves.

> Day 214: he thanked me out loud again, to a screen. I filed it under
> 'anomalies', same as the last 213 times.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
